## Recorded limitations

### bec

- Independent Float64 twin of the discrete 2D Gross–Pitaevskii operators (5-point Laplacian, harmonic trap, contact g, Visscher stagger, imag-time Euler with periodic L2 renormalization). Not a bit-exact GPU float32/float16 replay.
- Real-time norm evidence uses g=0 (linear Schrödinger limit); nonlinear dens at mixed half-levels is not claimed to conserve naive L2 exactly. Rotating-frame Ω L_z, Abrikosov lattice counting and turbulence are unvalidated.
- Thomas–Fermi μ/energy bands are asymptotic large-g checks; prepared single-vortex winding is not a noisy real-time vortex count or experimental Na BEC claim.
- Print evidence covers paused exportPNG state preservation and plate dimensions only; not lattice fidelity, calibrated color, or evolved/rotating exports.

### phyllotaxis

- Complete numerical and print evidence covers exactly the finite recipes and benchmark domain in validation/PHYLLOTAXIS.md.
- Larger parameter domains, longer histories, other hardware and physical or biological interpretations remain outside this completed label.
- Softened inverse-square discrete variant; sampled minimization, no biological or golden-angle universality claim. Interactive disturbance and grain are excluded.

### cyclic

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Complete evidence covers only the documented domain in validation/RDX.md and validation/results/rdx-science.json plus paused-initial print preservation in validation/results/rdx-print-state.json.
- Deterministic PDE only; no spiral wavelength or biodiversity-loss threshold of Reichenbach, Mobilia and Frey.
- No Float16 fallback, noise, nonlinear pattern-selection statistics, spiral/band spacing claims, evolved/running exports, or universal hardware claim.
- Broader settings require additional independent evidence.

### kpz

- Independent Float64 twin of column deposition kernels (random, relax, ballistic, RSOS) measuring W(t)~t^β; not a browser UI replay or Eden radial audit.
- Finite L=512 and five seeds per class; KPZ ballistic crossover often fits under 1/3. RSOS is the KPZ gate with a wider acceptance band.
- Family–Vicsek α at small L uses coarse bands only; no Tracy–Widom edge law or liquid-crystal experiment claim.
- No print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### sle

- The trace dimension disagrees with Beffara's min(2, 1 + kappa/8) at every tested point: at the tab's maximum N 8000 box counting gives 1.2236 +/- 0.0063 (kappa 2, -4.2 sigma), 1.3765 +/- 0.0076 (kappa 4, -16 sigma) and 1.4780 +/- 0.0055 (kappa 6, -50 sigma), rising slowly with N. The estimator recovers known dimensions on Koch curves of comparable size, so the shortfall is the uniform-capacity-time discretization of the tab's trace, not the driver or the model.
- Numerical agreement is established for the Loewner solver (zero, linear and square-root drivers, first order in dt, N 300-8000) and for the driver variance (kappa 2, 4, 6); the random curve's law beyond its box-counting dimension is not tested.
- Print evidence covers five recipes (kappa 2, 6, 8, the sweep and a 12-seed bundle) on one Chromium build; kappa values other than 2, 4, 6 appear only in print recipes.
- Finite samples from one seeded PRNG; error bars assume independent seeds.

### vegetation

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Complete evidence covers only the documented domain in validation/RDX.md and validation/results/rdx-science.json plus paused-initial print preservation in validation/results/rdx-print-state.json.
- Linear regime and uniform states only within this domain; float32 implicit denominator 1 + dt quantizes water loss (modelled in the discrete reference).
- No Float16 fallback, noise, nonlinear pattern-selection statistics, spiral/band spacing claims, evolved/running exports, or universal hardware claim.
- Broader settings require additional independent evidence.

### aztec

- Since 2026-09-24 the tab's frozen regions are the theorem's polar regions (a domino connected to the boundary through edge-adjacent dominoes of its own type, as Johansson 2005 states the definition of Jockusch, Propp and Shor); they equal an independent union-find on every plate, and the polar fraction extrapolates to 0.2141 +/- 0.0014 against 1 - pi/4 (-0.3 sigma). The earlier local test read 0.2778 +/- 0.0009, 67 sigma high.
- The status line's per-plate error bar (the spread of the four polar regions) is conservative: the scatter of the fraction over seeds is 0.68 to 0.98 of it at orders 40 to 320, because the four regions are anticorrelated. It fails the tool's calibration band (0.75 to 1.33, set before the data), so the record stays partially validated. At finite n the printed fraction exceeds the limit by a term that shrinks as n^(-2/3), and the status line says so.
- Exact uniformity is established by exhaustive enumeration only at orders 1 to 5, run through the tab's build() and shuffle() below the UI range of 8 to 320; larger orders are tested only through the arctic boundary statistics.
- The polar-region area and the axis radius agree with 1 - pi/4 and 1/sqrt(2) only after extrapolation in n with the stated form q_inf + A n^-2/3 + B n^-1 (0.3 and 0.7 sigma); a two-parameter n^-2/3 fit over orders 113 to 320 differs by 2.5 and 3.1 sigma, and at order 320 the polar area is still 0.030 above its limit.
- Uniformity probabilities assume the seeded U.makeRng streams behave as independent uniform draws; fixed PRNG samples do not prove it.
- Print evidence covers five recipes on one renderer (headless Chromium 141, SwiftShader, Linux). Grain is a raster finish that the vector print omits.

### rmt

- Complete evidence covers only the domain in validation/RMT.md: sampler, solver and exact moments for n 24-400 and beta 0.1-12, semicircle for beta 1-12, Gaudin-Mehta spacing law at beta 1 and 2 with n 400, the printed spread of two presets, Dyson mode at 24 paths, and five complete prints.
- The code samples the Dumitriu-Edelman matrix without its 1/sqrt(2), so the eigenvalue density is exp(-sum l^2/4); since 2026-09-24 the displayed equation says so, and it shows the Dyson mode's -l/2 Ornstein-Uhlenbeck drift at beta = 1.
- At n 150 the local unfolding (24-gap window) biases the spacing scale: the CV agrees with Gaudin-Mehta but the pooled KS distance (0.0043, 0.0044) exceeds its bootstrap critical value (0.0027, 0.0025). The spacing-distribution claim is limited to n 400.
- Since 2026-09-24 the status line prints the unfolded CV through compare() with its row-bootstrap error against the exact Gaudin-Mehta values (0.53435 at beta 1, 0.42426 at beta 2), with the Wigner surmise as a note; at beta 1 the tab's own sample resolves the surmise's 0.012 error.
