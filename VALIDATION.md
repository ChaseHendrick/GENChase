## Recorded limitations

### cyclic

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Registered evidence covers linear dynamics, uniform states, stencils and the cases named in tools/rdx-science.js on SwiftShader float32; it is not a parameter-domain, nonlinear-pattern or print-state audit.
- Deterministic PDE only; no spiral wavelength, no biodiversity-loss threshold of Reichenbach, Mobilia and Frey, noise term untested.

### vegetation

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Registered evidence covers linear dynamics, uniform states, stencils and the cases named in tools/rdx-science.js on SwiftShader float32; it is not a parameter-domain, nonlinear-pattern or print-state audit.
- Linear regime and uniform states only; no nonlinear band spacing or migration speed. The float32 implicit denominator 1 + dt quantizes the water loss rate by up to ulp(1)/(2 dt), modelled in the exact discrete reference.

### rogue

- The independent audit samples the exact analytic fields and checks the stated normalized NLS residual, peaks and periods; it does not integrate the browser solver or establish physical ocean accuracy.
- The finite plate samples a bounded window and does not prove the solution for every parameter or export resolution.

### fput

- Finite-time state evolution only; finest refinement step is below the UI minimum.
- The displayed E1 value is displacement amplitude, not energy. Periods kept is unused. Recurrence, equipartition and print accuracy are not validated.

### schrodinger

- Periodic Fourier and uniform-potential fixtures support the discrete recurrence, modified invariant and bounded second-order time/space refinement. Stability and conservation do not imply accuracy at coarse steps.
- Harmonic-bound, time-step edit, hard-wall and injection regressions cover stated float32 cases only. Native GPU trigonometric approximation is measured separately.
- Absorption, arbitrary potentials, long times, float16 and full physical scattering/print fidelity remain unvalidated. Corrections intentionally change affected historical images.

### excitable

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Registered evidence covers linear dynamics, uniform states, stencils and the cases named in tools/rdx-science.js on SwiftShader float32; it is not a parameter-domain, nonlinear-pattern or print-state audit.
- No spiral rotation period, meander or breakup statistics, no Barkley front speed (only its kinetics), noise term untested. A uniform excited Barkley cell stagnates near u = 1 in float32 (recorded finding).

### soliton

- The independent audit checks the exact Hirota two-soliton map against the KdV residual, mass and phase shift; it does not integrate the browser solver or establish physical shallow-water accuracy.
- The finite plate samples a bounded window and does not prove the solution for every parameter or export resolution.

### amb

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- No complete numerical convergence, parameter-domain or print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### cahn

- The deterministic constant/variable-mobility stencil is checked at declared grids and both boundaries; first-order temporal convergence is checked for one fixed-domain fixture. No spatial/continuum convergence claim.
- Additive forcing and clipping may change mass. Float16 fallback, long-time coarsening and all-parameter accuracy remain unaudited.
- Corrected variable mobility, effective timestep ceilings and click mixing intentionally change affected historical images or interaction results. Default declarations and seed interpretation are preserved.

### cgl

### vortex

- Winding counts, critical fields, screening, type-I/type-II transitions, hexagonal equilibrium, physical calibration and continuum convergence are not validated.

### breather

### turing

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Registered evidence covers linear dynamics, uniform states, stencils and the cases named in tools/rdx-science.js on SwiftShader float32; it is not a parameter-domain, nonlinear-pattern or print-state audit.
- Linear regime only; no nonlinear pattern selection (spots against stripes), no Hopf regime. The status-line wavelength is the onset-critical value 2 pi (Du Dv/det)^(1/4), 12 cells at the defaults, against 10.8 fastest-growing and 9.2 measured after saturation.

### tennis

- Finite parameter/time fixtures only; no exact flip-time, all-control or print accuracy claim.

### chemotaxis

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.

- Logistic growth means total mass is not conserved; the balance, not conservation, is tested. No aggregate shape or coarsening statistics, no analytic nonuniform steady state (none is available for this form), noise term untested.

### weierstrass

- The completed label covers only three explicitly enumerated finite field/print recipes for this module.

- The displayed finite Fourier sum is smooth. No nowhere-differentiability, fractal-dimension or high-frequency argument-accuracy claim is validated.

### convection

- Analytic component tests cover diffusion, conduction, free-slip velocity, Poisson inversion, buoyancy sign and signed instantaneous transport. These are not complete nonlinear Boussinesq validation.
- The Poisson benchmark uses 300 sweeps and does not certify the default four. No-slip convergence, high-Rayleigh resolution, half precision and temporal alignment of transport remain unaudited.
- The displayed Nu is sampled instantaneous transport, not a converged time average. Timestep/wall corrections intentionally change affected historical trajectories.

### reuleaux

- Validated only for the 13 recorded recipes and stated continuous-geometry, finite-field and caption-free PNG preservation claims. Not every parameter combination or external peer review.
- Width is measured from the complete sampled mathematical boundary, not the pixelated or possibly cropped picture. Geometry is known by construction, so this is a regression check, not a novel formula or independent physical prediction.
- Rotated overlays have no no-slip, force, contact or square-drilling model. Shape and width rose are finite-grid drawings.
- Print checks compare every native cell center, not every resampled edge pixel, perceptual color, PDF/TIFF conversion, captions/custom paper or physical output. More print pixels do not increase native scientific resolution.
- Four large-rose marks lie within the conservative rounding-error zone; the independent discrete support check finds no changed rounded locations for the recorded recipe. This is not a universal pixel-circle guarantee.
