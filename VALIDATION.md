## Recorded limitations

### rogue

- The independent audit samples the exact analytic fields and checks the stated normalized NLS residual, peaks and periods; it does not integrate the browser solver or establish physical ocean accuracy.
- The finite plate samples a bounded window and does not prove the solution for every parameter or export resolution.

### schrodinger

- Periodic Fourier and uniform-potential fixtures support the discrete recurrence, modified invariant and bounded second-order time/space refinement. Stability and conservation do not imply accuracy at coarse steps.
- Harmonic-bound, time-step edit, hard-wall and injection regressions cover stated float32 cases only. Native GPU trigonometric approximation is measured separately.
- Absorption, arbitrary potentials, long times, float16 and full physical scattering/print fidelity remain unvalidated. Corrections intentionally change affected historical images.

### soliton

- The independent audit checks the exact Hirota two-soliton map against the KdV residual, mass and phase shift; it does not integrate the browser solver or establish physical shallow-water accuracy.
- The finite plate samples a bounded window and does not prove the solution for every parameter or export resolution.

### amb

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- No complete numerical convergence, parameter-domain or print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### cahn

- The deterministic constant/variable-mobility stencil is checked at declared grids and both boundaries; first-order temporal convergence is checked for one fixed-domain fixture. No spatial/continuum convergence claim.
- Additive forcing and clipping may change mass. Float16 fallback, long-time coarsening and all-parameter accuracy remain unaudited.
- Corrected variable mobility, effective timestep ceilings and click mixing intentionally change affected historical images or interaction results. Default declarations and seed interpretation are preserved.

### ohta

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- No complete numerical convergence, parameter-domain or print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### hopf

- The independent audit checks inverse stereographic projection, sampled Gauss linking and orientation controls; it does not prove a continuous embedding for every parameter or validate the plate renderer.
- Numerical linking estimates are finite polygon quadratures and have no physical-unit interpretation.

### swift

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- No complete numerical convergence, parameter-domain or print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### pfc

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- No complete numerical convergence, parameter-domain or print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### ks

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- No complete numerical convergence, parameter-domain or print-state accuracy audit is registered.

### convection

- Analytic component tests cover diffusion, conduction, free-slip velocity, Poisson inversion, buoyancy sign and signed instantaneous transport. These are not complete nonlinear Boussinesq validation.
- The Poisson benchmark uses 300 sweeps and does not certify the default four. No-slip convergence, high-Rayleigh resolution, half precision and temporal alignment of transport remain unaudited.
- The displayed Nu is sampled instantaneous transport, not a converged time average. Timestep/wall corrections intentionally change affected historical trajectories.

### reuleaux

- The independent audit samples the actual circular-arc construction and checks constant-width convergence and area; it does not validate rolling dynamics or the browser print path.
- Support widths are sampled at finite angular resolution and the area check is polygonal.

### apollonian

- The independent audit checks the integer Descartes reflection construction, tangency and finite-generation area; it does not prove the infinite gasket limit or validate the browser renderer.
- The displayed packing is bounded by generation and curvature/circle limits, so a finite nonzero gap is expected.

### double-triangle-bound

- Classical vortex model; these tests do not establish historical novelty.
- Kernel and trajectory tests cover declared samples, not all parameters or print rendering.

### maxwell

- Periodic lossless TMz fields only, stationary positive epsilon and uniform mu; no absorption, PML, losses, dispersion or nonlinear materials.
- Analytic modes and one float64 dielectric slab fixture support the recorded cases; discontinuous-interface scattering and geometry convergence remain unvalidated.
- Float32 is required. High-grid evidence is four-step sanity only; long-time, hardware and complete parameter coverage remain open.

### molecular

- Two-dimensional periodic force-shifted Lennard-Jones model, with no thermostat or named material/EOS claim.
- Force, linked-cell and bounded trajectory checks support stated fixtures only; the local stiffness/energy guard is not a global nonlinear stability proof.
- Print checks preserve evolved particle state and compare raster/SVG output; broad devices, parameters and long-time thermodynamics remain unvalidated.

### surfaces

- Established parametric surfaces with finite transparent wire meshes, not physical soap-film simulations or new mathematics.
- Independent differential geometry and mesh checks cover declared samples; no global topology, complete immersion, self-intersection or all-parameter certification.
- Printed coordinates agree across raster/SVG, while antialiasing varies by backend; arbitrary devices remain untested.

### plasma

- One-dimensional electrostatic periodic CIC electrons with immobile uniform ions; physical energy is approximate. No validated Landau damping, nonlinear instability threshold, collisions or magnetic physics.
- Bounded fixtures and selected print states do not certify every allowed setting, long evolution or device.

### shallow

- First-order wet flat-bottom two-dimensional Saint-Venant finite volumes. No dry-bed flooding, relief, physical viscosity, dispersion or real-world flood forecast. Numerical diffusion broadens shocks.
- Bounded fixtures and selected print states do not certify every allowed setting, long evolution or device.

### nonreciprocal

- Periodic finite-difference Heun implementation of Saha–Golestanian2025 Eq5, not the paper spectral solver or a reproduced phase diagram. Conditional timestep scale is not a nonlinear stability proof. Prepared waves are imposed.
- Bounded fixtures and selected print states do not certify every allowed setting, long evolution or device.

### hodgkin-huxley

- The independent audit covers the stated squid parameters, selected currents and finite recording durations; it does not validate every control, temperature, biological preparation or human application.
- Print checks preserve numerical state and dimensions but do not calibrate colors or establish experimental accuracy.

### neural-mass

- The evidence covers selected analytic, driven and preset trajectories of the infinite all-to-all MPR reduction; it does not validate every control, finite-neuron network or experimental brain recording.
- Print checks preserve traces and dimensions but do not create temporal resolution beyond the stored samples.

The remaining records retain each catalog equation and reference as a review target. They do not
claim those descriptions have been checked against the primary paper. Full parameter, precision,
boundary-condition, convergence and print-state reviews remain outstanding where recorded.
