## Recorded limitations

### phyllotaxis

- Complete numerical and print evidence covers exactly the finite recipes and benchmark domain in validation/PHYLLOTAXIS.md.
- Larger parameter domains, longer histories, other hardware and physical or biological interpretations remain outside this completed label.
- Softened inverse-square discrete variant; sampled minimization, no biological or golden-angle universality claim. Interactive disturbance and grain are excluded.

### cyclic

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Registered evidence covers linear dynamics, uniform states, stencils and the cases named in tools/rdx-science.js on SwiftShader float32; it is not a parameter-domain, nonlinear-pattern or print-state audit.
- Deterministic PDE only; no spiral wavelength, no biodiversity-loss threshold of Reichenbach, Mobilia and Frey, noise term untested.

### vegetation

- Catalog equation and citation are review targets, not verified paper equivalence.
- Registered evidence covers linear dynamics, uniform states, stencils and the cases named in tools/rdx-science.js on SwiftShader float32; it is not a parameter-domain, nonlinear-pattern or print-state audit.
- Linear regime and uniform states only; no nonlinear band spacing or migration speed. The float32 implicit denominator 1 + dt quantizes the water loss rate by up to ulp(1)/(2 dt), modelled in the exact discrete reference.

### ust

- Complete evidence covers only the finite recipes and benchmark domain in validation/UST.md
- Ideal independent random draws underlie the uniformity theorem; fixed PRNG frequencies do not prove it. No SLE scaling claim.
- Broader settings require additional independent evidence.

### rogue

- The completed label covers the enumerated recipes in validation/ANALYTIC-FIELD-REVIEW.md, not every parameter or renderer.
- These are analytic solution samples, not independent time integration, physical experiments or a global proof. Finite grids and windows can miss peaks, mass or energy tails.
- PNG enlargement adds no field resolution. Other exports, captions, smoothing and printer calibration are outside this review.

### fput

- Only the finite fixed-end alpha-chain equations, histories, diagnostics and native PNG recipes in validation/FPUT-FIELD-REVIEW.md.
- No long nonlinear recurrence, equipartition, arbitrary-amplitude cubic-potential stability, continuum limit or physical experiment certification.
- The historical periods key now denotes elapsed physical time; corrected integration and energy diagnostics intentionally change old recipe output.

### schrodinger

- Harmonic-bound, time-step edit, hard-wall and injection regressions cover stated float32 cases only. Native GPU trigonometric approximation is measured separately.
- Absorption, arbitrary potentials, long times, float16 and full physical scattering/print fidelity remain unvalidated. Corrections intentionally change affected historical images.
