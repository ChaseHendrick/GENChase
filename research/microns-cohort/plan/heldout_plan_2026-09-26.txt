HELD-OUT ANALYSIS PLAN, MICrONS cohort ("higher-order") excess.
Written 2026-09-26, about 16:40 UTC by the machine clock. Written before any statistic was computed on the new axons. Up to
this point only file names, column headers and row counts of scratchpad/neuro-data/microns-heldout/ have been read.
This plan is not changed after the held-out numbers are seen. Any deviation, and the reason for it, is recorded in
heldout_deviations.txt with its time. The sha256 of this file is recorded in heldout_plan.sha256 when the file is
written.

0. PURPOSE
The test asks whether postsynaptic partners of a presynaptic axon are more similar in function to one another than a
pairwise connection model predicts, on axons that were not in Ding et al. 2025's 148. The model is the one that
reproduces the main-data result in scratchpad/neuro-data/microns-fixes. The claim under test, and the only claim a
positive result supports, is: structure remains in which cells each axon contacts that no pairwise model with free
per-axon and per-cell propensities (and the stated pair covariates) reproduces. The size of the excess is a test
statistic. It is not the size of a cohort rule. On synthetic data, the degree-preserving null absorbs 74 to 89% of an
injected anchor-type rule (see section 7).

1. DATA
- Held-out: edges_new_axons_connected_adp_v1822_part*.csv (625 axons, materialization v1822).
- Reference: edges_ding_axons_connected_adp_v1822_part*.csv (Ding's 148 axons), with co-travel recomputed by the same
  pipeline. Every step below is applied to the reference exactly as to the held-out table.
- Nodes: Ding et al.'s node_data_v1.pkl (responses, readout weights, RF positions, soma positions, layer, hva, CC).

2. ROWS, CELLS AND UNITS (identical for both tables)
a) Rows: population C (connected) and A (ADP, axon-dendrite proximity without synapse). Same-region rows are not used;
   the analysis is at synaptic scale.
b) Drop rows with post_excl29 == 1 (the 29 cells Ding et al. excluded everywhere).
c) Both cells must be in node_data_v1 and pass cc_max_cvt > 0.4 and cc_abs_cvt > 0.2 (Methods).
d) dend_len > 0 is required on every C and A row. A C or A row with dend_len <= 0 or missing is an error.
e) Hard errors, checked before any statistic:
   - duplicated (pre, post) rows;
   - a presynaptic cell with C rows but no A rows;
   - an overall A:C ratio below 5;
   - nucleus coordinates outside the nm range of node_data_v1;
   - hva labels other than V1 and HVA;
   - for the held-out table only: any presynaptic cell shared with Ding's 148.
f) Projection type comes from the node hva labels of the presynaptic and postsynaptic cells (V1->V1, HVA->HVA,
   V1->HVA, HVA->V1).
g) The unit is a (projection type, presynaptic cell) group. Groups with no C row are dropped.
h) Pair covariates are computed from node_data_v1:
   - sil: Pearson correlation of in_silico_resp;
   - viv: Pearson correlation of in_vivo_mean_resp;
   - fsim: cosine of readout_cvt;
   - rfd: approximate RF distance, sqrt((99.81 dx)^2 + (55.14 dy)^2) of position_stim_cvt. On the release it has
     Pearson 0.998 with Ding's column and median absolute error 0.17 deg. It is used for both tables alike.
   - logsoma: log of the soma distance in um, floored at 1 um.
i) Postsynaptic depth bins for N3: the six bin edges frozen from the main analysis (work/bin_edges.npy).

3. ELIGIBILITY
A group is eligible for the cohort statistic when it has at least 10 distinct connected partners in its table, as in
Ding et al. Fig. 5c and the main analysis. The eligible set is fixed at the observed data. In the bootstrap, the same
groups are kept, and the rule re-applied to each resample is recorded as a sensitivity.

4. STATISTIC
For each eligible group i: rho_i = sum over distinct cells j != k of N_ij N_ik S_jk, divided by sum over j != k of
N_ij N_ik, with S the signal correlation.
excess = mean over eligible groups of (rho_i observed - E_null rho_i).
Primary: the digital-twin measure (S = sil) under the dt covariate family, pooled over projection types, null N1.

5. NULLS (cx.py, frozen; code hashes in heldout_plan.sha256)
- N1 (primary): every postsynaptic cell's total synapse count and every group's total are held fixed. Synapses are
  reassigned by Metropolis swaps inside the proximity pools, with pair weight L exp(x b).
  - b comes from the two-way (group and cell) fixed-effect Poisson fit, with covariates (sil, fsim, rfd) x projection
    type, refitted on each table.
  - Chains for the point estimate: 2 chains of 300 burn-in sweeps plus 1000 draws, thin 3.
- N3 (key secondary): N1 plus logsoma x projection type, and each group's count in each postsynaptic depth bin held
  fixed.
- N0 (stage-1 comparator): a multinomial over the own pool with p ~ L exp(x b), with group fixed effects only.
- In vivo (secondary): measure viv, family (viv, fsim, rfd), nulls N1 and N3.

6. INFERENCE AND DECISION RULE
a) Two-way bootstrap, B = 500.
   - Presynaptic cells are resampled with replacement and enter as weights. Postsynaptic cells are resampled with
     replacement and enter as distinct copies. Eligible groups are fixed.
   - Every model is refitted in each replicate, with short swap chains (30 burn-in sweeps + 25 draws, thin 2).
   - SE_boot is the SD of the replicates.
   - In the coverage study on synthetic data, this bootstrap was conservative (SE ratio about 2 with and without a
     rule), so it is the primary basis for the decision.
b) PRIMARY DECISION: the held-out result replicates when the N1 excess (dt) has z_boot = excess / SE_boot >= 1.645
   (one-sided alpha 0.05). Otherwise it does not replicate. The same rule applied to the reference table is reported
   next to it.
c) Also reported, with no bearing on the decision:
   - the conditional z, excess / SD of the pooled rho over the null chain's draws, which ignores uncertainty in b and
     misspecification;
   - 95% percentile and normal intervals;
   - N3 and in vivo, each with the same rule;
   - N0;
   - per projection type, with a Holm adjustment over 8 tests (4 types x 2 measures) under N1;
   - the difference between held-out and reference, with SEs combined as independent.
d) If a chain fails, or a fit fails to converge in more than 2% of replicates, that is reported, not hidden.

7. CALIBRATION REPORTED ALONGSIDE
- The absorption curve from the synthetic runs on the real design: injected anchor-rule strength gamma, then the
  oracle excess against the true pairwise model, then the excess recovered by N0, N1 and N3.
- The coverage and SE ratio of the bootstrap on the synthetic super-population.
- Negative controls (no rule): realistic postsynaptic heterogeneity, laminar targeting, and generators outside the
  null's family (overdispersed multi-synapse counts, axon-specific laminar preference).
- Caveat stated with any result: a positive N1 excess shows structure beyond any pairwise model with free per-cell
  propensities. It is not an effect size. A null result is weakly informative, because N1 recovers only about 12 to
  26% of an injected anchor rule.

8. WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
- The 625 axons are outside Ding et al.'s set. They are not provably proofread after Ding's snapshot.
- Co-travel comes from substitute (pcg_skel) skeletons, so ADP is more inclusive than Ding's. That is why the
  reference is the recomputed Ding table, not edge_data_v1.pkl.
- One animal.
