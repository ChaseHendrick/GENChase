# Independent review of prospective quality-standard adoption

The current checker is admitted for the narrow prospective bookkeeping scope.
It requires one visible 2026-10-03 adoption section, one matching standard
declaration, exactly seven sequential U1-U7 titles matching the established bar,
completed boxes and nonempty evidence. It does not establish the scientific
truth of the evidence. Historical archive checks preserve their prior behavior.

The initial implementation accepted a fenced example, an HTML-comment example,
reversed U order and U7 after an H1 or tab-separated H2 section boundary. Those
actual isolated fixtures were reported and the author corrected the parser.
The final independent run passed 16 cases, including these negative controls,
unclosed fences/comments, an actual adoption plus a quoted template, an allowed
H3 subsection, stale dates, an open U6 item and a hidden standard declaration.
The current original checker self-test passed 62 cases.

The prospective standard, publishing instructions and cardiac addendum retain
the seven historical titles and explicitly keep new obligations open. They
distinguish source/proof review, native production, summary checking and complete
independent witness replay. Conditional operator/orbit premises remain explicit;
a failed six-state sufficient certificate supplies no instability or count-zero
conclusion. In-project independent review is described accurately and does not
claim outside human peer review. No old archive is retrospectively promoted to
the stronger standard.
