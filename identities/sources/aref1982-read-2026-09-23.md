## What Aref proves/states (from abstract + secondary citation only — not a body read)

From the abstract alone:

- Discrete symmetries of the initial vortex configuration are preserved by the equations of motion.
- A **center of symmetry** (overlooked vs classical multi-axis cases of Gröbli / Greenhill) yields an **effective two-body Hamiltonian integrable** problem (vs effective one-body when several axes of symmetry are present).
- Example: **double alternate ring** configurations (circular analogs of the Havelock vortex street).
- Fully nonlinear mode: double rings **asymptotically dissolve into freely moving vortex pairs**.
- Closing discussion: relevance to disintegration of vortex streets in 2-D flow.

Secondary literature (e.g. Koiller et al. 1985, which *extends* Aref) attributes conjectures on opposite-circulation two-ring equilibria radii and three-ring nonintegrability to Aref; Banica–Miot and others cite **Koiller 1985** for constructed two-ring collapses. None of that secondary material supplies Aref’s 1982 equations or an optimized pitch floor from Aref’s own pages.

**Not verified from Aref body (unread):** relative-equilibrium classification details, collapse vs dissolution distinction in the paper’s own notation, any spiral-pitch / product-\(P\) formulas, any minimization over angle.
