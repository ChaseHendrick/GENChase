## What the paper actually does

1. **Center of symmetry** preserved by the point-vortex equations → reduction to an effective **Hamiltonian two-degree-of-freedom** system with an extra integral → **integrable** (Propositions on \(z,\zeta\) pairs).
2. Steady **double alternate ring** configurations (Havelock); analytic \(\xi_n\) for small \(n\); Table I of \(r/\rho\) ratios for \(n=2,\ldots,20\).
3. Fully nonlinear mode: double rings **asymptotically dissolve into freely moving vortex pairs** (potential \(U_n\), trapping, pair size / impact parameter).
4. Discussion: relevance to **vortex-street disintegration** in 2-D flow; Karman / Havelock limiting street; degeneracy of assembling a street from a double alternate ring.

This is **street / pairing / integrability** physics, not the classical opposite-circulation **two-ring self-similar collapse** product floors studied in GENChase / Koiller §11 / Gotoda.
