## What the paper actually does

1. **Scope.** Three concentric regular \(m\)-gons with possibly different circulations \(\Gamma_j\) (optionally a fixed central vortex). Relative equilibria = uniform rotation (constant distances). Collapse = homographic shrink with complex rate parameter \(\omega\) (nonzero real part → finite-time collision at origin while rotating).

2. **Main theorems.** Prop. 5: for generic circulations (with listed nondegeneracy conditions), the number of triple-ring **relative equilibria** is finite. Prop. 6: for fixed \(\omega\) with nonzero real part, the number of **collapse** configurations is likewise generically finite. (Varying \(\omega\) continuously can still give a continuum of collapses.)

3. **Methods.** Algebraic geometry / projective configurations; reduced polynomial systems (L1)–(L4) simpler than raw ODEs (2.3). §5 plots zero-sets in radius ratios to locate equilibria numerically.

4. **Examples (Figs. 1–5).** Numerical \([w_1,w_2,w_3]\) for selected \(\Gamma\); Fig. 5 collapse with \(\omega \propto 1+(4/9)i\), circulations \(\pm 1\). No closed-form spiral-pitch minimum.

5. **Two-ring prior art (not optimized floors).** Intro (p. 123): “collapse configurations of two rings are known to exist [7]” with [7] = Koiller et al., Physica D 16 (1985). That cites the classical family already read as **DOES NOT KILL** in `koiller1985-read-2026-09-23.md`. This paper does not re-derive or sharpen Koiller’s rates into \(F_n\).
