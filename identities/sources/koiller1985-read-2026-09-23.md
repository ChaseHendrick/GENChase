## §11 Collapse motions (pp. 59–60) — what is present

Journal page header in extract: *“J. Koiller et al./ On Aref’s vortex motions with a symmet… center 59”*.

**Virial / circulation condition for two equal rings of \(n\) vortices** (OCR/extract of (11.1)–(11.2)):

> In general, a necessary condition for such solutions is that \(\sum X_j = \sum h_j^2\) … In the present case we have \(n\) vortices of strength \(k\), \(n\) of the strength \(h\). Thus … \(n[(n-1)(k^2+h^2)+2nkh]=0\) \(\Leftrightarrow\) \(d=0\) (\(\tau=\tau_{K,L}\)). (11.2)

**Proposition 12** (quote from extract):

> Proposition 12. Let \(\tau=\tau_L\) and \(J=0\).  
> a) Symmetric and alternate steady rotating solutions coexist at rings ratio \(x_-\) given by (3.6).  
> b) All other solutions either escape to infinity at rate \(t^{1/2}\) or collapse to the origin in finite time. **Collapse trajectories in physical space are logarithmic spirals** (as guessed by Aref [15]).

**Radial rate \(a(\phi_0)\) and spiral form** (11.4)–(11.5), OCR-noisy but content clear:

- \(r^2 = a(\phi_0)\,t + r_0^2\) with \(a(\phi_0)\) proportional to \(\sin(n\phi_0)\) over a denominator with \(\cos(n\phi_0)\) (extract: \(a(\phi_0)= 2n\lambda_1|\tau_L|^{n/2}\sin n\phi_0\big/\big(1+|\tau_L|^n-2|\tau_L|^{n/2}\cos n\phi_0\big)\)).
- Angular rate \(A\) from (3.15), constant when \((r/p)^2=1/|\tau|\).
- Spiral: \(r = r_0\exp\bigl((a(\phi_0)/A)\,(\phi-\phi_0)\bigr)\) (11.5).

Rates are given **as functions of a fixed angle** \(\phi_0\). No \(\min_{\phi} |A/a|\), no product floor, no equality case.

Section ends immediately into **§12 Discussion** (no further collapse algebra).
