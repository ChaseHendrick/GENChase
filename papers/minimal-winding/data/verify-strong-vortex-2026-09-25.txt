Checks of Theorem 3 (a strong vortex with m weak tight pairs). Parts 1 and 2 are exact or check the explicit
bounds of the proof; parts 3 and 4 are numerical and illustrate the theorem without proving it: P > sqrt(3)/2
at the fixed gamma used here is a numerical observation (Proposition 4 proves it only below a threshold that is
not explicit; verify_pairs_bound.py checks that proof).
