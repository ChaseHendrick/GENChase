# Manuscript figure and layout audit, 2026-09-29

The manuscript previously contained result tables but no figures. Figure 1 now gives a visual entry point to the section geometry and the stored certified crossing calculation, without adding a numerical trajectory or a new proof claim.

- Panel (a) plots the 23 stored h-set centres in `configs/horseshoe_E0.cfg`. Angles are reduced to [-pi, pi); the momenta retain the manuscript's dimensionless m = l = g = 1 convention. No points are connected, no manifold is inferred, and the tiny h-set widths are not drawn at this scale. These centres are numerical design values, not certified locations of a trajectory.
- Panel (b) draws the two rectangular image enclosures printed for candidate pieces in `data/E0.txt`. The generator parses the original endpoints and verifies that the stored report says all checks passed and that both stored slope intervals are strictly negative. The plotted rectangles alone do not prove transversality, and their overlap does not assert overlap of the actual images. The caption makes those limits explicit.
- The figure is a vector PDF with embedded fonts, a 6.5-by-3.25-inch source canvas, readable axes and coordinate scales, and different line styles as well as colors for the boxes. Its source manifest records the input and generator SHA-256 hashes, parsed endpoints, and library versions.

Reproduce with `python3 -m pip install -r code/requirements-figures.txt` and `python3 code/make_figures.py` from the companion root. Figure dependencies are optional and separate from the proof dependencies. The program needs no network, random seed, solver, CAPD build, or new simulation. Two consecutive runs produced byte-identical figure PDFs. The files in `data/` and `configs/` are unchanged.
