# Numerical figure and layout review, 2026-09-29

A new four-panel vector figure shows voltage and the three gating variables at 18.5 C and 6.3 C. This covers the two temperatures of the existence theorems without suggesting stability or uniqueness. It uses the printed-leak `hp_pulse_<T>_El10.613.json` records, not the older zero-current `pulse_<T>.npz` initial guesses. The time coordinate is shifted to the sampled voltage maximum, each temperature has its own clearly labeled time scale, and neither asymptotic tail is extrapolated.

`python3 code/make_figure.py` uses the existing `hhwave.Wave` equations with explicit `EL=10.613`. Each of 49 short DOP853 segments starts at its committed high-precision shooting node, avoiding whole-orbit amplification of floating-point errors. The endpoint disagreement, tighter-tolerance comparison, source SHA-256 values, package versions and failure control are recorded in `data/figure-profile-checks.json`. Switching to the historical zero-current leak is deliberately tested and rejected by the same endpoint thresholds. These are display-quality numerical checks, not interval proof evidence or a global error bound. Sampled extrema in that file are extrema of the plotted samples, not certified extrema of the continuous pulse.

The maximum voltage endpoint mismatch is below 4.7e-9 mV, and the voltage difference after tightening the local tolerance is below 2.5e-8 mV on the tested samples. The vector figure PDF was byte-identical on a repeat run in the tested environment. It appears with an explicit numerical-illustration caption on page 5 of the 15-page manuscript.

The manuscript was rebuilt with the repository's `tools/paper-build.sh` and Tectonic. Figure pages were inspected visually at normal page scale. The final PDF contains the public research contact, ORCID and the separate end-matter rights notice, with no extracted replacement glyphs or undefined-reference markers. The paper checker passed. No proof programs or existing certificates were changed or recomputed. Existing scientific limitations remain in place.
