# Paper 2 scouting: self-similar collapse without rotation (kappa real) in the alpha-models

Scratch work, 2026-09-25. Nothing here is in the repository. Law: dz_j/dt = (i/2pi) sum_k G_k (z_j - z_k)|z_j - z_k|^(-alpha-2);
gauge 2 pi kappa = -1 + i b (b = 0 on the zero-winding set Z), collapse point at 0. Every result is labelled
**PROVED**, **NUMERICAL** (binary64, KKT/Newton residuals ~1e-12, not interval arithmetic) or **FAILED**.
Priority is unconfirmed for everything; no new literature search was made (see RESEARCH.md P, Q).

## Method used throughout: the threshold is a constrained minimum

The recorded threshold of a family (where its least P first reaches 0 as alpha rises) is a local minimum of
alpha on Z = {kappa-real collapses, alpha free}, a manifold of dimension N - 1 (N - 2 at fixed alpha). Its KKT
system (equations, J^T lambda = e_alpha) is square, so Newton on it gives alpha*(N) to ~1e-12 instead of the
1e-4 brackets, and a positive-definite reduced Hessian certifies a strict local minimum (numerically).
`code/polish_thresholds.py` does this for all 69 recorded thresholds: every polished value lies inside its
certified bracket, KKT residual <= 4e-12, reduced Hessian positive definite (`data/thresholds_polished.json`).
Because the KKT system is square and nondegenerate, paper 1's Krawczyk pipeline could certify any alpha*(N)
rigorously (not done here).

Searches (all end at the values above):
- continuation in alpha of all 13 recorded certified minima at N = 9, 10 (alpha = 0, 1, 2) up to P = 0, then min alpha on Z (`more_starts.py`);
- random walk on Z with large perturbations (positions, circulations, sign flips) + min alpha: 5352 attempts / 323 landings at N = 9, 5221 / 277 at N = 10 (`perturb_search.py`);
- vortex removal homotopy from the N = 11 collapse (drive G_k -> 0 while minimizing alpha): all 10 weak vortices give 2.0913751011; the strong negative one fails (`removal.py`);

Since alpha*(10) = 2.0914 > 2 and P(alpha = 2) is linear in alpha* - alpha, the N = 10 family misses zero winding by a clear margin. This is evidence, not a proof that no 10-vortex kappa-real collapse exists at alpha = 2.

**Key question.** Along the known families alpha*(N) is positive and decreasing at every N computed (least values 0.9644 at N = 73
for S, 0.9824 at N = 79 for the Euler C2 branch) and extrapolates to about 0.66-0.80 for both: the form
a + b/(ln N)^q, which tracks the drifting local exponent, gives 0.66-0.68, polynomial and power forms 0.72-0.80;
fits that force alpha_inf = 0 are 50-1000 times worse in rms. The Euler (C2) branch lies above S, approaches it,
and shows the same late behaviour: its N^2 |d alpha*/dN| stops falling at 19.24 near N = 70 and turns up. This is consistent with no Euler collapse without rotation along
these families, but it says nothing about other families, and a sub-logarithmic approach is not excluded by
N <= ~70.

## (iii) The 9-parameter family at alpha = 2, N = 11

- **NUMERICAL.** At the certified point the 22 equations have full rank in 31 unknowns (sigma_min/sigma_max 7e-4): tangent dimension 9. Continuation along both signs of all 9 tangent directions (`explore_family.py`) runs to arclength 2-4 (relative to the configuration) with no collision, no vanishing circulation and no loss of rank; the sign pattern (one opposite-sign strong vortex, ten of the other sign) never changes and the circulation span varies from 22 to 106.
- **PROVED (elementary).** No member has reflection symmetry. If a configuration is invariant under a reflection that keeps circulations, the law gives V_{pi(j)} = -conj(V_j) (for the real axis), and V = kappa z forces kappa = -conj(kappa), a relative equilibrium. Reflection with G -> -G is compatible with kappa real but forces sum G = 0 and no vortex on the axis, so N even (and paper 1's class excludes sum G = 0).
- **NUMERICAL.** No C5 member: two C_n rings and a centre reduce to one scalar equation (ring-2 circulation forced to -1/rho^2), which has no collapse solution for n = 2, 3, 5 at alpha = 1, 2 (`rings.py`). C2 members (centre + 5 pairs) exist, but the least alpha found for them is 3.7369117731 > 2, so none at alpha = 2 was found. Removing any weak vortex from the family ends at alpha = 2.0914 > 2.
- **FAILED.** No simplest member or closed form. Making the nine weak circulations equal (8 conditions) does not close: the most equal member found has weak circulations within a factor 1.32 (SLSQP stationary; Newton on the 8 extra equations stalls at residual 2e-2).
